# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Project skeleton: single-instance config flow, English and Italian translations, placeholder brand images, development environment (dev container, Home Assistant and ha-mcp in Docker) and CI (tests against the stable and beta Home Assistant, security, hassfest, Marketplace validation, CodeQL).
- Inventory database (SQLite in the configuration folder, numbered migrations) with zones, products, essentials, stock and a journal of every movement.
- Actions `add_zone`, `remove_zone`, `adjust_stock`, `undo_last`, `set_stock`, `add_product`, `set_essential`, `export_data` and `import_data` (JSON full backup, CSV stock list), with English and Italian texts. Undo reverts a whole operation, even when it changed several products.
- Shopping list todo entity with automatic items for essentials below minimum, manual and suggested items, optional purchase recording when checked, one-way copy to another todo list and import from a todo list.
- Sensors for essentials to buy, purchase suggestions (with the reason) and expiring products; best-before calendar; Go shopping and Undo buttons; update mode select; options flow.
- Actions `get_suggestions`, `refresh_shopping_list`, `start_shopping`, `add_to_shopping_list` and `import_from_todo`.
- LLM tools for conversation agents using the Assist API (stock, missing, suggestions, expiring, shopping list, adjust stock with clarifications, undo, essentials and new products with confirmation).
- Action `ask` for Alexa and other channels: sessions per channel with clarifications, reply or acknowledgement within 5 seconds, reprompt; options for the agent, session length and read-only Alexa.
- Image analysis of zones with any AI Task entity: quality checks, crop, resize and metadata removal with Pillow, skip of unchanged images, structured or text output, validation.
- Proposals of changes with confirmation, corrections and alias learning; automatic mode above a confidence threshold; actions `analyze_image` and `confirm_pending`, LLM tool to confirm; sensors for pending proposals and daily analyses.
- Websocket commands for the dashboard (snapshot, live updates, journal, photo upload) and a write permission rule for non-admin users; undo of a given operation.
- Zone manager: sources in priority order (camera with crop and daily limit, manual), zone checks with fallback to a photo request, cron schedules, reminders for zones without camera, photo requests with one reminder and expiry; actions `set_zone` and `check_zone`, LLM tool to check a zone; per-zone last check sensor, zone error binary sensor and check button, photo requests sensor.
- camera_show: products shown to a zone camera recorded as consumption (or returned), burst capture with sharpness, change and duplicate checks, suspension with a repair issue; show mode switch per zone, last shown sensor, `arm_show_mode` action and LLM tool.
- Passage cameras: products carried in or out are proposed, and corroborate zone camera sightings without double counting.
- Confirming only some items of a proposal keeps the others pending; an accepted below-target suggestion buys up to the target; the dashboard snapshot includes zone checks, errors, show mode and photo requests.
- Telegram channel through the Telegram bot integration: texts to the agent per chat, photos to the vision pipeline with zone from the caption or buttons, proposal and suggestion buttons, undo, commands, allowed chats, group option and rate limit.
- Notifications per message type with fallback entities, quiet hours and optional Telegram copies with buttons: photo requests, zone checks, expiring products (daily), camera recognitions with Undo, essentials running out, shopping list and suggestions, weekly summary, late voice replies.
- Alexa guide (`docs/alexa.md`) and examples: multilingual interaction models and `intent_script` for `quartermaster.ask`, generated by `scripts/alexa-models` with one intent per starting word so the whole sentence reaches Quartermaster, and yes/no answers per language.
- `ask` does not speak tool calls that a model wrote as text (and says the request was not fully carried out), and a request waits for the previous one of the same session instead of failing.
- Guided setup: name and language, AI models, first zones with optional camera and crop, Telegram and voice, notifications, first essentials and an optional import from a todo list.
- Language option for the messages sent by Quartermaster.
- Options grouped in a menu (general, voice and Alexa, shopping list, Telegram, notifications, permissions).
- Diagnostics with personal data redacted (only counts and settings).
- Receipts: `analyze_receipt` action, Telegram caption or button, dashboard upload; purchases from order emails through the IMAP integration. Both become purchase proposals.
- Consumption forecast: suggestion reason "running out" and essentials on the list before they run low.
- Interior cameras checked when their door closes; shelf life sets the best-before date of purchases.
- Kept crops for proposals and zones, served to the dashboard; zone settings in the snapshot for zone editing.
- AI Task for text, hourly analysis limit, per-minute limit for `ask`, 15 s camera timeout, tool argument validation, "counted" quantities by voice, photo requests that say what to frame, per-zone announcements, failed checks announced.
- Automation examples.
- README and user guide.

### Changed
- Cameras never write (author decision). A product taken in front of a camera becomes a request on Telegram and in the dashboard, with buttons for the quantity; it is recorded as a consumption only on a yes, with Undo. A product put back cancels a request still waiting or asks to undo the confirmed consumption; goods placed with nothing to undo are not loaded (they enter with a receipt or a photo sent on purpose). Passage cameras only support a zone sighting or ask about goods carried out. The source option `confirm` is gone (ignored if stored).
- A camera's count of a zone is always a proposal, of decreases only, even in automatic update mode; products seen in a larger quantity are reported, not loaded.
- Telegram waits for the answer and sends one message instead of "I'm on it" and a second one; Alexa no longer promises a later message when no destination for late voice replies is set.
- List check by voice: "and then?" ("e poi?", "continua") reads the next five products; new Alexa intents in the interaction models (import them again).
- Telegram `/check` (`/controlla`) without a zone, as sent by the bot's command menu, offers the zones as buttons.
- Goods a camera sees being put away that were bought in the last 24 hours now only confirm the shopping: one camera message sums up what was put away ("Shopping put away: Milk 3 seen of 3 bought") and reports a difference without changing the stock; event `quartermaster_show_put_away`. Goods with no recent purchase still ask for the receipt.
- Unknown products in a photo or receipt no longer vanish when the proposal is confirmed: they stay waiting and expire with it. Each can be created as a new product, named as read or as given, with the quantity seen: its "➕ New" button on Telegram (a receipt asks for the zone), the Proposals card, or the correction `new_product` (with `name`, and `zone` for a receipt) in `confirm_pending` and the agent's tool. `confirm_pending` now also returns the keys still `waiting`, and takes `moved` (the quantity taken) to correct a camera request.
- Products recorded in a zone that a photo or camera count does not show are proposed at zero as "not seen". Confirming the proposal leaves them alone; each one is set to zero only with its own Telegram button ("<product> finished"), by ticking it in the dashboard or by naming its key.

### Added
- Product life cycle: actions `update_product` (rename, aliases, category, unit, default zone, shelf life, visual hints, essential settings; null clears), `archive_product` (stock to zero as one undoable operation; `archived: false` or adding the same name brings it back) and `move_stock` (between zones, one undoable operation, the best-before date goes with it), and agent tools for them (changes and archiving ask for a yes; moving does not). The dashboard snapshot adds `shelf_life_days`, `visual_hints` and `archived_products`.
- List check (source type `ledger`, also offered in the guided setup) for any zone a photo shows badly (a closed cupboard, the fridge, a freezer): no photo requests and no "zone not updated" suggestion; the check sends the list, oldest first, with "−1", "finished" and "all right" buttons on Telegram, every `check_days` (default 30, 0 = on request), with `/check <zone>` (`/controlla`), the check button or by voice (the tool returns the five oldest products).

### Fixed
- The agent's tools and `quartermaster.ask` follow the write permissions: a user who may not write gets `not_allowed` instead of changing the inventory through Assist.
- A Telegram message or a camera trigger is no longer handled again when its entity comes back with its restored state (restart, reload of the bot).
- A checked automatic shopping item no longer comes back at once: the product stays off the list until its stock changes or two days pass. Completed items are deleted after 30 days.
- A product finished and bought again without a date no longer keeps the old best-before date and opening time (undo restores them).
- camera_show compares frames with an uncropped reference, so the change score is right when the source has a crop; two triggers at the same moment no longer both capture.
- Open dashboards keep receiving updates after the entry reloads.
- A zone checked with an unchanged picture counts as updated, keeps its waiting proposal instead of adding a copy, and sends no second notification; a new count of a zone replaces the one still waiting. A product listed twice by the model is summed.
- An essential already at zero no longer makes every camera check end in a photo request.
- Photo reminders for zones without camera wait `reminder_days` after an unanswered request instead of repeating daily.
- Consuming or finishing a product in a named zone where it has no stock is refused with `not_in_stock` instead of recording an empty movement.
- The Undo button under a camera notification undoes that movement, not the camera's latest one.
- Quiet hours no longer hold answers to something the user just asked (a photo request to the asking chat, a late voice reply) and are validated in the setup and options forms.
- Expiry and "finished on" dates use the local day; Telegram errors are in the chat's language; malformed buttons are ignored.
- A product whose name is made of digits is found by name; JSON import validates dates and statuses and reports database errors; oversized images are refused cleanly; a model answering `"false"` as text is understood.
- The list sensors keep their `items` attribute out of the recorder; temporary images left by a crash are deleted at startup.
